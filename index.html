About Me

Technical depth, strategic intent.

I'm a mining professional passionate about integrating data, design thinking and strategic leadership into modern mining operations. My career spans drill and blast, technical services, geomechanics and sustaining capital projects across Western Australia, Queensland, the Philippines and Kenya.

I'm currently Mine Manager at Yilgarn Iron Ore, leading the Parker Range open-pit iron ore operation from care and maintenance back to full production. I hold WA statutory credentials and an Executive MBA from UNSW Business School's Australian Graduate School of Management (AGSM).

Outside work I contribute to community development, youth mentoring and educational initiatives that empower the next generation.
